Oquirrh Mountain Girls Softball

About & Board

Our mission

To provide a quality recreational softball program by teaching the fundamentals of softball in a safe and enjoyable atmosphere. To provide opportunities to improve softball skills and learn the value of good sportsmanship while enhancing self-esteem.

Our seasons

OMGS runs three seasons each year: the Spring season (generally March through May), All-Stars in the summer (June 1 to early August), and the Fall season (generally August through October).
