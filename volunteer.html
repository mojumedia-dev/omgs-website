Volunteer & Coach

Coach with OMGS

Our coaches are volunteers, and we could not do this without you.

  • Head coaches, assistant coaches, and anyone helping on the field must complete an online coaching application before the first game.
  • The head coach and one assistant must complete a background check release (through D1 Prospects).
  • Head and assistant coaches must be at least 21, unless the Board grants an exception.
  • Every team needs at least one approved adult female (coach, team parent, or chaperone) at every practice, game, and team activity.
  • All coaches complete an online Child Safety Training class before coaching.
  • Coaches and assistants attend the mandatory coaches meeting and clinic in March and help at the pitching and players clinics.
  • A coach's daughter must be listed as a coach pick for the coach to be selected.

Coach applications for Spring 2027 are coming soon. Questions? Email contact@omgsoftball.org.

Snack Shack

Each team provides one volunteer (14 or older) to work the BBQ at its scheduled times, listed on your team schedule. These times will not conflict with your games. If a team does not provide a volunteer, the team forfeits one game.

Other ways to help

  • Team parent
  • Scorekeeper (GameChanger). The parent meeting is highly recommended for scorekeeping training.
  • Centennial Park clean-up day
  • Board service. When a seat opens, OMGS posts it and interviews applicants.

Questions? Email contact@omgsoftball.org.
