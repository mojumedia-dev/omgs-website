Fields & Location

OMGS plays at three parks: Centennial Park (our home field), Tuscany East, and Western Springs.

Centennial Park

13000 South 2700 West, Riverton, UT 84065

Field Status

If games are canceled for weather, they are rescheduled at the earliest possible time.

Spring Preseason Practice Slots

Before spring games start, coaches reserve one-hour practice slots. Slots start Monday to Thursday at 4:30, 5:30 and 6:30 PM, Friday from 4:30 to 7:30 PM, and Saturday from 9 AM to 2 PM.

How to sign up

Coaches sign up through the league's Google Sheet, which the board shares with coaches before the season.

Practice sign-up sheet

  • No practice on Centennial Fields A, B and C after games start. All other fields are first come, first served after games start. Please be considerate of others.
  • OMGS provides coaches with a permit for all fields. Have it with you any time you practice at Western Springs or Tuscany. For permit forms, contact Adam Lloyd, OMGS President, at adam@omgsoftball.org.
  • Fall: there are no reserved slots. Fields are first come, first served at Tuscany, Western Springs and the Grass Bowl.
