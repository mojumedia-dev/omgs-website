Oquirrh Mountain Girls Softball

Fields & Location

OMGS plays at three parks: Centennial Park (our home field), and Tuscany East.

Centennial Park

13000 South 2700 West, Riverton, UT 84065

Directions (Google Maps) Directions (Apple Maps)

Diamonds

Field A, Field B and Field C.

T-Ball Bowl

T-Ball plays on a diamond set up in the grass bowl east of the pavilion.

Grass Bowl

Northeast, Northwest, Southwest, and Southeast corners. Same address.

Snack Shack

First aid kit and ice are available at the snack shack.

Tuscany East (Tuscany Park)

5469 W Toscana Way (12745 South), Herriman, UT 84096

Directions (Google Maps) Directions (Apple Maps)

Field Status

If games are canceled for weather, they are rescheduled at the earliest possible time.

Spring Preseason Practice Slots

Before spring games start, coaches reserve one-hour practice slots. Slots start Monday to Thursday at 4:30, 5:30 and 6:30 PM, Friday from 4:30 to 7:30 PM, and Saturday from 9 AM to 2 PM.

Practice locations

How to sign up

Coaches sign up through the league's Google Sheet, which the board shares with coaches before the season.

Practice sign-up sheet

  • No practice on Centennial Fields A, B and C after games start. All other fields are first come, first served after games start. Please be considerate of others.
  • OMGS provides coaches with a permit for all fields. Have it with you any time you practice at Tuscany. For permit forms, contact Adam Lloyd, OMGS President, at adam [at] omgsoftball [dot] org.
  • Fall: there are no reserved slots. Fields are first come, first served at Tuscany and the Grass Bowl.
