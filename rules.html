Key general rules

  • OMGS games follow D1 Prospects rules (our affiliated governing organization) plus OMGS playing, division, and tournament rules. See the 2027 D1 Prospects Rules (PDF).
  • Game time is forfeit time. Ten defensive players, open substitution, and teams bat everyone present.
  • No player sits out two innings in a row.
  • Eight girls are needed to start a game (except T-Ball/6U).
  • Half innings end at three outs or a run limit: five runs (8U and 10U), seven runs (12U, 14U, 18U).
  • Regular season games can end in a tie.
  • Line-ups go into GameChanger at least 10 minutes before game time.
  • No intentional walks. Leading off is allowed after the ball leaves the pitcher's hand.
  • Metal cleats are allowed only in 14U and 18U.
  • A player must play in at least 75% of her games to be eligible for All-Stars. T-Ball/6U players are not eligible.
  • Both teams clean up their own dugout and bleacher area after every game. Wait for the team leaving the dugout before your team goes in.
  • A coach who is ejected from a game must miss the next game, if it is not played the same day.

Safety

  • Batting helmets with masks must be worn by any girl batting or running bases during a practice, practice game, or game, and stay on until she is back in the dugout.
  • All infielders must wear fielder's face masks during games and practice games.
  • If a player is bleeding during a practice or game, it must be bandaged with no visible blood, or she cannot continue.
  • Every player must have a signed concussion form with her registration to be eligible to play in any game.
  • The league provides a uniform shirt and socks. League uniforms may not be altered in any way. If your whole team wants to do something special with its shirts, get Board approval first.

Sportsmanship

OMGS has a zero tolerance policy for abusive behavior. No derogatory or abusive remarks, including foul language, toward any coach, player, spectator, or umpire. This can lead to suspension, ejection from the park, or forfeit. Positive cheers are encouraged. Chants against the other team, especially the pitcher, are not okay.

Year-end tournament

  • Single elimination, seeded by league win/loss record (tie breakers: head to head, runs allowed, run differential, runs scored).
  • Games are 60 minutes for 8U, 10U, and 12U (no new inning after 55) and 70 minutes for 14U and 18U (no new inning after 65).
  • Ties go to one International Tie Breaker inning, then put outs, then hits.
  • The first tie breaker inning starts with the last batter from the previous inning on second base.
  • Complete line-up cards with each player's first and last name and uniform number go to each team's scorekeeper at least 10 minutes before game time.
  • Home team: the higher ranked team in the winner's bracket; a coin toss at the pre-game conference for consolation games; the undefeated team in the championship game; a coin toss for the "if" game.
  • The home team keeps the official score in GameChanger. The visiting team provides one person to run GameChanger as a backup.
  • Teams play 10 on defense with free substitution (4 outfielders and 6 infielders). Outfielders stay on the grass until the ball crosses the plate. Players must play defense at least every other inning, and teams bat their whole roster.
  • 8U exception: all players may play defense (6 infielders and 6 or more outfielders). All infielders stay behind the 35 foot safety chalk line until the ball crosses the plate.
  • Protests are ruled on by the umpire and the board member on duty. Judgment calls cannot be protested. All decisions are final.
  • The handbook's written protest process: the protest goes to the age group director with a $25 non-refundable fee. Protests on judgment calls, protests filed incorrectly, and protests on matters that don't follow the rules are invalid.

Division rules

Full rules for each division, from the OMGS Rules page and the 2026 Coaches Handbook. Tap a division to open it.

T-Ball / 6U rules

  • Ages: 3 through 5 years old prior to Sept 1 of the previous year.
  • Ball: 11 inch safety softball.
  • Game length: 60 minutes or three innings.
  • Field: T-Ball has no diamond. It plays on the T-Ball grass bowl.
  • Games start at game time no matter how many girls are there. No score is kept.
  • Teams switch after the whole lineup has batted. Players advance one base on a ball hit, and the last batter and base runners run until all have crossed home plate.
  • Defense: all players play defense each inning.
  • Pitching: a coach pitches up to five balls to each batter. If she doesn't hit it, the coach puts the ball on the tee and she gets three swings off the tee. No walks.
  • Coaches on the field: only three coaches are allowed on the field.
  • Not eligible for All-Stars.

8U rules

  • Ages: 8 or younger prior to Sept 1 of the previous year. A girl cannot play down.
  • Ball: 11 inch. No new inning after 55 minutes.
  • Distances: pitcher's plate 35 feet, bases 60 feet.
  • Run limit: half innings end at three outs or five runs.
  • Defense: all players play defense each inning. Six players are in the infield in standard positions and the rest play outfield (grass). The infield is the area inside the baselines. For safety, all players start behind the 35 foot arch line until the batter hits the ball. The outfield arch is on the grass. The umpire decides whether the ball is in the infield or outfield for base running and time out.
  • Defensive coach: one defensive coach may be on the field (in the outfield, on the grass) for instruction only. Defensive coaches are encouraged to stay off the field in the second half of the season, or whenever the Board asks. If the coach intentionally touches a live ball or moves a defensive player by physical contact, the batter-runner gets the base she is running to plus one. If it happens again, the coach leaves the field. No defensive coach is allowed on the field during the year-end tournament.
  • Pitching: a coach pitches underhand to their own team and must stay in contact with the pitching rubber while pitching.
  • Coach-pitcher interference: if the coach-pitcher intentionally interferes with a batted ball or a play, the batter-runner is out and all runners return to the base they held at the pitch. If it happens again, the coach leaves the field. If the ball hits the coach-pitcher while getting out of the way, the ball is live.
  • The coach-pitcher may talk to batters or runners only between pitches (no live-ball coaching from the mound), including while waiting for the ball back from the catcher. No delays catching the ball to coach from the mound. Use your base coaches.
  • The coach-pitcher or base coach may not physically help runners stop or go. A runner contacted this way is out.
  • Pitches and strikeouts: a batter gets five pitches. Five unhit pitches or three strikes is an out. A foul on the third strike or fifth pitch is not an out. If a batter fouls off pitches and then lets a pitch go by, she is out. No dropped third strike rule. No walks.
  • Base advancement: batters may advance one base on a ball hit in fair territory in the infield. The batter and runners may advance one more base on a misplayed ball or overthrow, at their own risk. After the first defensive play and advance, runners stop until the next hit ball. Two bases is the most allowed on a ball that stops or is stopped in the infield and overthrown. A runner cannot advance home on an overthrow.
  • Runners may score only on plays that result from a hit ball. Example: a runner on 2nd goes to 3rd on a hit, then the ball is overthrown or misplayed. She may not go home.
  • Balls hit to the outfield: players may advance more than one base at their own risk until the ball is thrown to the infield (the base lines between bases). Then the ball is dead and runners stop at the base they are going to. Runners who must return do so after time is called. If a play is made on a runner after the ball is back in the infield and the ball is misplayed, the runner gets one base (1st to 2nd or 2nd to 3rd). If a runner on 3rd can't go home, other runners can't go past 3rd. Only one overthrow or misplayed ball per batted ball.
  • Stealing: not allowed. No advancing on a wild pitch, passed ball, or throw back to the pitcher.
  • Infield fly: the infield fly rule is eliminated.
  • Bunting: no intentional bunting.

10U rules

  • Ages: 9 or 10 prior to Sept 1 of the previous year.
  • Playing up: the old rules page said a girl "cannot play up or down" in this division, which conflicts with the general playing-up rule. The general rule applies: girls cannot play down, and playing up is case by case with board review (she then cannot play back down and must re-request each season). See Divisions.
  • Ball: 11 inch. No new inning after 55 minutes.
  • Distances: pitcher's plate 35 feet, bases 60 feet.
  • Run limit: half innings end at three outs or five runs.
  • Defense: the outfield arch for defensive placement is on the grass.
  • Base running: runners may advance as many bases as they like, at their own risk of being put out on an overthrow.
  • Dropped third strike: applies all season.
  • Stealing: 2nd, 3rd, and home are allowed after the ball leaves the pitcher's hand.
  • Coaches may not physically help runners stop or go. A runner contacted this way is out.
  • Pitching: no coach pitch. Girls pitch under the regular rules (the old rules page cites NFHS; D1 Prospects and OMGS rules now govern).
  • Hit batter rule: if the pitcher hits two batters in the same inning, the pitcher is warned. At three hit batters in the same inning, it is up to the coach whether to leave the pitcher in. After four hit batters in the same inning, the pitcher must come out of the pitching position for that inning. She may return to pitch the next inning.
