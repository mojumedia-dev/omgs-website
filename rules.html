Key general rules

  • OMGS games follow D1 Prospects rules (our affiliated governing organization) plus OMGS playing, division, and tournament rules. See the 2027 D1 Prospects Rules (PDF).
  • Game time is forfeit time. Ten defensive players, open substitution, and teams bat everyone present.
  • No player sits out two innings in a row.
  • Eight girls are needed to start a game (except T-Ball/6U).
  • Half innings end at three outs or a run limit: five runs (8U and 10U), seven runs (12U, 14U, 18U).
  • Regular season games can end in a tie.
  • Line-ups go into GameChanger at least 10 minutes before game time.
  • No intentional walks. Leading off is allowed after the ball leaves the pitcher's hand.
  • Metal cleats are allowed only in 14U and 18U.
  • A player must play in at least 75% of her games to be eligible for All-Stars. T-Ball/6U players are not eligible.
  • Both teams clean up their own dugout and bleacher area after every game. Wait for the team leaving the dugout before your team goes in.
  • A coach who is ejected from a game must miss the next game, if it is not played the same day.

Safety

  • Batting helmets with masks must be worn by any girl batting or running bases during a practice, practice game, or game, and stay on until she is back in the dugout.
  • All infielders must wear fielder's face masks during games and practice games.
  • If a player is bleeding during a practice or game, it must be bandaged with no visible blood, or she cannot continue.
  • Every player must have a signed concussion form with her registration to be eligible to play in any game.
  • The league provides a uniform shirt and socks. League uniforms may not be altered in any way. If your whole team wants to do something special with its shirts, get Board approval first.

Sportsmanship

OMGS has a zero tolerance policy for abusive behavior. No derogatory or abusive remarks, including foul language, toward any coach, player, spectator, or umpire. This can lead to suspension, ejection from the park, or forfeit. Positive cheers are encouraged. Chants against the other team, especially the pitcher, are not okay.

Year-end tournament

  • Single elimination, seeded by league win/loss record (tie breakers: head to head, runs allowed, run differential, runs scored).
  • Games are 60 minutes for 8U, 10U, and 12U (no new inning after 55) and 70 minutes for 14U and 18U (no new inning after 65).
  • Ties go to one International Tie Breaker inning, then put outs, then hits.
  • The first tie breaker inning starts with the last batter from the previous inning on second base.
  • Complete line-up cards with each player's first and last name and uniform number go to each team's scorekeeper at least 10 minutes before game time.
  • Home team: the higher ranked team in the winner's bracket; a coin toss at the pre-game conference for consolation games; the undefeated team in the championship game; a coin toss for the "if" game.
  • The home team keeps the official score in GameChanger. The visiting team provides one person to run GameChanger as a backup.
  • Teams play 10 on defense with free substitution (4 outfielders and 6 infielders). Outfielders stay on the grass until the ball crosses the plate. Players must play defense at least every other inning, and teams bat their whole roster.
  • 8U exception: all players may play defense (6 infielders and 6 or more outfielders). All infielders stay behind the 35 foot safety chalk line until the ball crosses the plate.
  • Protests are ruled on by the umpire and the board member on duty. Judgment calls cannot be protested. All decisions are final.
  • The handbook's written protest process: the protest goes to the age group director with a $25 non-refundable fee. Protests on judgment calls, protests filed incorrectly, and protests on matters that don't follow the rules are invalid.
