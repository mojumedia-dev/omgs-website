Privacy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This page explains how the Oquirrh Mountain Girls Softball (OMGS) website handles information.

No accounts on this website

You do not need an account to use this website. We do not ask you to sign in or create a profile here.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, for example which pages are visited and general device and location information. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this.

OMGS uses this information only to improve the website, and OMGS does not sell it. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services (opens in a new tab). To stop Google Analytics from collecting your visits, you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on (opens in a new tab).

If you close the announcement bar at the top of the page, your browser remembers that choice on your own device so the bar stays closed. That choice is not sent to OMGS.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them.

Forms on this website

The Play Like a GIRL nomination form, the Board Member Application, and the League Family Services submission form are Google Forms. OMGS uses what you submit only for that form's purpose: reviewing nominations, reviewing board applications, or listing a family service.

League Family Services listings

Listings on the League Family Services page show the business and contact information that providers submit.

Player registration

Player registration is handled by our registration platform, Stack Sports, under its own privacy policy (opens in a new tab) and terms (opens in a new tab).

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and OMGS does not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. Parents and guardians handle player registration.

Contact

Questions about this page? Email contact [at] omgsoftball [dot] org.
