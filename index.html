What is Oquirrh Mountain Girls Softball?

Oquirrh Mountain Girls Softball (OMGS) is a recreational girls softball league based in Riverton, Utah. It is for girls ages 3 to 18.

  • Divisions: T-Ball/6U, 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, and 18U, based on birth month and year. See the age chart and division finder.
  • Seasons: Spring (generally March through May), summer All-Stars (June 1 to early August), and Fall (generally August through October). Every team is guaranteed 10 games each season.
  • Fields: Centennial Park in Riverton is the home field. Teams also play at Western Springs Park in Riverton and Tuscany East in Herriman. See fields and directions.
  • How to register: use any Register button on this site. It opens Sports Connect, where you sign in or create an account. Spring registration usually opens in mid-January.
  • Contact: contact [at] omgsoftball [dot] org.

01 · SKILLS

Taught right, from the ground up.

Fundamentals in a safe and enjoyable setting, with a free coaches clinic, free preseason pitching clinics, and players clinics ($20 for OMGS players, $30 for players not registered with OMGS, in 2026).

02 · CHARACTER

Teammates first. Always.

Good sportsmanship, positive cheers, and zero tolerance for abusive behavior from anyone at the park.

03 · COMMUNITY

A whole park behind every girl.

Players from 20+ cities across Salt Lake and Utah County, plus volunteer coaches, team parents, BBQ shifts, and clean-up days. Fun, growth, and friendships that last all season.

Three seasons

Spring. Summer. Fall.

Spring

Spring season

Our main league season, generally March through May. Dates may change each season. Registration usually opens in mid-January. Players attend a skill assessment (tryout) before draft day, then teams are drafted. Coach picks do not need to attend.

Summer

All-Stars

June 1 to early August, for selected 8U to 18U players. All-Stars have their own tryout and draft, separate from spring.

Fall

Fall season

Generally August through October. Fall teams: each coach may freeze up to six players in total, including 2 pitchers and 1 catcher. OMGS then builds the rest of each team, keeping teams as balanced as we can by looking at experience, positions, and more.

Get involved

It takes a whole park.

Coach

Lead a team. Online application, background check, and Child Safety Training required. Applications open soon.

BBQ assignments

BBQ assignments: each team is required to cover one 2-hour BBQ shift during the season. In both Spring and Fall, if a team does not cover its BBQ shift, the team forfeits its next game.

Umpires

D1 Prospects handles umpires for 10U and up. Brooke Olsen, our Vice President, handles 8U umpires: brooke [at] omgsoftball [dot] org.
