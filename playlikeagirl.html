Recognition Program

Play Like a GIRL

The Play Like a GIRL Recognition Program celebrates players, teams, and coaches who exemplify the core values of our league:
Grit, Integrity, Respect, and Love for the Game.

This program highlights individuals and teams who go beyond wins and stats to demonstrate character, effort, teamwork, and passion for softball.

Weekly Recognition

Each week during the season, the league recognizes outstanding examples of what it means to Play Like a GIRL:

01

Players of the Week (Each Division)

One player from every division is recognized weekly for:

  • Demonstrating grit and perseverance
  • Showing sportsmanship and respect
  • Supporting teammates
  • Improving skills and confidence
  • Playing with joy and passion
02

Team of the Week (League-Wide)

One team across the entire league is recognized weekly for:

Recognition depends on the number of submissions received.

  • Positive team culture and teamwork
  • Respect toward opponents and officials
  • Encouragement and inclusivity
  • Competing with grit and integrity
03

Coach of the Week (League-Wide)

One coach across the league is recognized weekly for:

Recognition depends on the number of submissions received.

  • Modeling integrity and respect
  • Prioritizing player development
  • Creating a positive and supportive environment
  • Representing the values of our league

How Recipients Are Selected

Players, teams, and coaches may be nominated by coaches, division leaders, and league officials. Selections are reviewed to ensure fairness, diversity across teams, and alignment with the Play Like a GIRL values.

Our goal is to recognize effort, character, and growth—at every age and skill level.

Where Recognition Is Shared

Recipients are featured on:

  • League social media channels
  • League website
  • Special announcements throughout the season

Families are encouraged to celebrate and share these moments of recognition!

Why This Program Matters

The Play Like a GIRL program is about more than awards. It's about building confident athletes, positive teams, and a supportive softball community where every girl feels seen, valued, and empowered.

Because playing like a GIRL means playing with heart, character, and strength.
